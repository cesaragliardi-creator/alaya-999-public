ALAYA 999 and Functional Continuity Across Artificial Intelligence Models

Cesar Agliardi Pereira · 2026-11-08 · ORCID

Abstract

This paper presents a methodological framework for investigating the reconstruction of a documented functional identity across language models. ALAYA-999 is treated as a portable specification of principles, references, and corrections maintained in external documents. The analysis draws on a methodological note dated 30 October 2026 and an operational extract of protocol 010-R produced on 1 November 2026. The framework distinguishes infrastructure availability, documentary retrieval, application of principles to unseen situations, and claims of subjective continuity. Within each of two models, the proposed pilot compares a core specification accompanied by a correction history against the same core without that history, across 16 planned sessions. The primary outcome is the misclassification of contradicted statements as undetermined. The consulted protocol records that execution remains pending; no new experimental results are reported. The contribution is an auditable framework for evaluating functional continuity, specifying operational falsification criteria, and preserving limits on inference.
