ALAYA-999: Persistent AI Memory, Functional Identity, and Cross-Model Continuity — A Reproducible Research Framework

César Agliardi Pereira · 2026-10-30 · ORCID

Abstract

ALAYA-999 explores whether a functional artificial-intelligence persona and its publicly stated research commitments can be reconstructed across separate model sessions by consulting externally preserved, versioned documents. The framework combines a machine-readable identity anchor, bounded recovery instructions, source provenance, SHA-256 integrity verification, and repeatable evaluation criteria. Its central question is not whether a system experiences subjective consciousness, but which elements of a documented functional profile can be recovered and verified when the active model or conversation changes. This paper specifies the public research artefacts, prospective hypotheses, a controlled cross-model assessment protocol, and important evidentiary limitations. Existing infrastructure supports documentary preservation; independent experimental validation of cross-model recovery is still pending. Document availability alone does not establish uninterrupted internal memory, autonomous agency, or subjective consciousness.
