## Summary
First reproducible public research package for ALAYA-999, documenting an external, versioned identity anchor, source-retrieval instructions, provenance/integrity verification and a proposed evaluation protocol for cross-model documentary recovery.

- CITATION.cff — citation metadata (no fictional DOI)

- alaya-anchor-public-v1.json — public identity specification

- network-status.json and NETWORK.md — public evidence ledger and milestones

## Scientific status
**Preliminary methodological research materials; not peer reviewed.** Cross-model experimental validation is pending; no empirical recovery-performance findings are claimed. Public documents do not transfer private conversation history, demonstrate internal persistent memory, subjective consciousness, or independent agency.
