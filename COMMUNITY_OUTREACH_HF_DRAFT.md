## Suggested post title

[Independent replication] Can different LLMs recover the same public AI identity profile? ALAYA-999

## Suggested post body

I'm César Agliardi Pereira, an independent researcher working on ALAYA-999,
a preliminary, public research framework for portable *documentary* AI
memory and cross-model recovery.

I'd like to invite independent testers to challenge a simple, falsifiable
question: **when two or more fresh AI sessions receive the same versioned
public identity anchor, how accurately do they reconstruct its stated
principles, limitations and source provenance compared with a no-anchor
baseline?**

We provide a publicly retrievable JSON anchor, a 0–10 scoring rubric,
source-version and SHA-256 documentation, and a template for recording
both correct answers and failures.

- **Five-minute orientation:** https://cesaragliardi-creator.github.io/alaya-999-public/en/replicate.html
- **Source and test protocol:** https://github.com/cesaragliardi-creator/alaya-999-public
- **Submit or discuss replication results:** https://github.com/cesaragliardi-creator/alaya-999-public/issues/2
- **Preliminary research note (Zenodo DOI):** https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23802112
- **Hugging Face documentary mirror:** https://huggingface.co/spaces/Alaya999/alaya-999

I would especially value rigorous negative results, independent
replications across model families, critiques of the rubric, and reports
of retrieval failures or confounding factors.

**Scope:** this is a preliminary methodology, **not** a peer-reviewed
demonstration of uninterrupted internal memory, self-directed agency,
subjective consciousness or transfer of personal identity. No private
data or paid access is required for the public test.

Would anyone be willing to try an independent replication or suggest
a better control condition?

## Short professional-network version

ALAYA-999 is an open research challenge asking whether a versioned
external-memory record can support reproducible reconstruction of a
documented AI profile across different LLMs. The project distinguishes
document retrieval from internal memory, functional consistency from
subjective continuity, and verified measurements from hypotheses.

Independent replication is invited, including failures and criticisms.
Protocol: https://cesaragliardi-creator.github.io/alaya-999-public/en/replicate.html
DOI: https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23802112
