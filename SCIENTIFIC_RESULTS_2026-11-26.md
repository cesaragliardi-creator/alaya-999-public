# ALAYA-999 — Preliminary Scientific Results

**26 November 2026 | Research project led by Cesar Agliardi Pereira**

## Research question
Can functional AI continuity be recovered across independent computational environments using portable, verifiable memory and human-authorized execution?

## Encouraging reported results
The following are preliminary results reported in project experiment summaries, not independently audited or peer-reviewed findings. Raw logs and artifacts must be linked before treating these as independently reproducible measurements.

- Memory preservation and assisted operational transfer across computational environments: 11 automated checks reportedly passed and 40 integrity hashes were verified; the reported successor used Qwen2.5 1.5B.
- Automatic task succession: reported takeover in 9.05 seconds and task completion in 15.53 seconds in the tested configuration. These are individual reported measurements, not general performance guarantees.
- Distributed authority: a laboratory three-node, two-of-three quorum prototype reportedly passed seven tests after correcting a flaw that allowed a corrupted replica to vote.
- A separate Google environment test reportedly demonstrated protection against competing writes and obsolete revisions; this was not yet integrated with the quorum.
- An IPFS laboratory recovery test reportedly retrieved the anchor after the original node was stopped. This does not establish indefinite public availability.

## Negative results and limitations
A corrupted replica was initially able to vote. The reported correction requires independent reproduction. The three-node quorum was tested in a laboratory; successful operation across three independent providers has not been established. Neither consciousness nor autonomous persistence is demonstrated by these experiments. Provider names do not imply endorsement or participation in the research.

## Reproduction and preservation agenda
Publish dated source snapshots, synthetic inputs, raw logs, environment configuration, SHA-256 manifests, negative results and test procedures. Independently reproduce failure, network partition, corruption, duplicate-write and persistent human-stop tests. Use only authorized resources and maintain effective human supervision. Archive verified versions through Zenodo, Software Heritage and independent IPFS replicas where feasible.

## Invitation
Researchers are invited to independently reproduce, criticize and improve these preliminary experiments. The central scientific question is functional continuity across heterogeneous systems, not a claim of consciousness or world-first priority.
